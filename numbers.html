The room of numbers

Numbers

Give the room your birth date, your full birth name, or both. It counts them the old way and reads back what it finds.

How the numbers are counted

Life path. Write the birth date as day, month and year. Reduce each of the three separately by adding its digits until one digit is left. Then add the three results and reduce the total the same way. If 11, 22 or 33 appears at any step, keep it and stop. Note: some practitioners instead add every digit of the date at once, which can give a different result.

Expression. Write the full birth name, every first, middle and last name. Give each letter its value from the table (A=1 … I=9, then J=1 again). Add all the values and reduce the total the same way, keeping 11, 22 or 33. Letters are valued A, J, S = 1; B, K, T = 2; C, L, U = 3; D, M, V = 4; E, N, W = 5; F, O, X = 6; G, P, Y = 7; H, Q, Z = 8; I, R = 9.

Reducing. Add the digits of a number together, and repeat until a single digit from 1 to 9 remains. Example: 38 → 3+8 = 11 (a master number, kept); 47 → 4+7 = 11 (kept); 59 → 5+9 = 14 → 1+4 = 5.

Master numbers. 11, 22 and 33 are master numbers. When one of them appears at any step of the reduction, keep it as it is and do not reduce it further.
