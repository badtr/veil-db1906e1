Why this album was made

The Unreturning

In his own hand

Five songs for the road that none walks back again:a lake of drinks, an offering table laid,a throne that is wept over, and thenthe dead left free at last, no longer stayed.

Beneath, the old ritual voices go;above them, one of porcelain, alone;and what goes down to the water, so,does not come home. It does not come home.

— M.A.
