What the Fire Kept

In his own hand

Two nights around a single fire;the drums, the stones, the blood; the crowdthat wants them burned upon the pyreand calls for it, and calls aloud.

And two who will not die dance throughthe flames, and on through them they tread,then lie down, side by side, the two,in the warm ash, as in a bed.

— M.A.

From the album notes

The first night

Two nights around one fire

Two nights around one fire. Drums, stones, blood and a crowd that wants them burned. Two who will not die dance through the flames and lie down together in the warm ash.

The second night

What the fire kept

The drums return, then fall silent. Only ash and wind remain. They lie down together in the warm ash and sleep. They do not die.
