Why this album was made

The Face-Down

In his own hand

Five songs for those found face down where they lay,beneath the ink; the cut that came to stay;the dead, borne home and carried all the way:these five songs before them now I lay.

Dark, close and ritual, they are sungby a porcelain voice, and from its tonguenot once is any glance turned aside:it does not look away from those who died.

— M.A.
