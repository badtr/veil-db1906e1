Of these cards

Why I made this deck

I practise the dark arts. Within this hallthe Porcelain Ballerina has her place:this house keeps the record of it all,the art behind it, and its every trace.

The twenty-two great trumps I drew anew,more dark, more dangerous to behold,and yet each card still holds its meaning true,the meaning that it carried from of old.

So sit, and ask. The reading laid for youis meant to be as true, as wholly true,as any that a living reader reads,card after card, as each one leads.

— M.A.
